Social Media Marketing:

The social media marketing services rendered through TopMBAStudy can be designed to showcase the distinct features of your institution. We can use platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and other relevant channels to bring your institution's strengths to prospective students.

Content Marketing:

Students spend significant time researching colleges and courses before making an academic decision. TopMBAStudy can help ensure that your institution becomes part of that research journey through a range of content formats.

Field Marketing Initiatives

Some of the on field educational advertising strategies that can be used to strengthen your institution's brand recognition include:

EXPOS AND EDUCATIONAL FAIRS

Create direct opportunities to interact with students and build institutional connections.

PRINT MEDIA ADVERTISING

Use newspapers, magazines, brochures, and other relevant print formats.

TRANSIT ADVERTISING

Build wider geographical visibility through selected public transportation touchpoints.

INFLUENCER MARKETING

Work with relevant creators, bloggers, industry experts, and education voices.

COLLABORATIONS WITH LOCAL INSTITUTES

TARGETED SURVEY ANALYSIS

Gather audience insights and feedback to understand student preferences and improve campaigns.

05

Expos and Educational Fairs:

Educational fairs, exhibitions, seminars, and student focused events can provide an opportunity for institutions to directly interact with prospective students and strengthen their presence within the education community.

06

Print Media Advertising:

Newspapers, magazines, brochures, and other print formats continue to provide opportunities for institutions to build awareness among relevant local audiences. Print campaigns can be planned around institution, programme, admission, or event focused communication.

07

Transit Advertising:

Transit advertising can provide geographical flexibility and broad audience visibility by placing institutional messaging across selected public transportation touchpoints.

08

Influencer Marketing:

Relevant social media personalities, bloggers, industry experts, and education voices can help institutions communicate their strengths through a more relatable and authentic format.

09

Collaborations with Local Institutes:

Academic and promotional collaborations with schools, colleges, training institutions, and other educational communities can help extend an institution's reach and create meaningful connections with prospective students.

10

Targeted Survey Analysis:

Student feedback can help institutions understand audience preferences, interests, and areas of improvement. Survey based insights can support better campaign planning and help track engagement, website traffic, enquiry patterns, and other performance indicators.
